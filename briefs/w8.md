2. Every h3 section of the existing articles present in the same order.
3. Tab content preserved from the md source, monospace-safe inside <pre>.
4. Curly quotes in prose; facts and hedges unchanged from the md.
5. File written to fragments/l38-article.html and nothing else touched.

## Output
