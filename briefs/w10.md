# Brief w10 — Reddit post bank for the free lessons

Read `AGENTS.md` in the workspace root first. Its voice and honesty rules apply.
This brief overrides its file-format section: deliverables are markdown content
drafts, not lesson files.

## Job
Draft Reddit posts promoting the free lessons on guitar.solutions. Reddit despises
marketing and rewards genuinely useful free things shared by their maker. Every
post is written as the author (Jason, writing as Johnny Suede) sharing his own
work, with authorship disclosed plainly and zero sales language.

## Inputs (read for voice and facts)
- fragments/reference/lessons.html (the four free lessons as deployed)
- README.md
- ptq-01-jeff-buckley-tone-hallelujah.html (voice sample)

## Facts you may state (nothing beyond these)
- Four complete lessons are free at guitar.solutions: Purple Haze, Comfortably
  Numb, Pride and Joy, Smells Like Teen Spirit. No email, no signup.
- Each free lesson has its own page: guitar.solutions/lesson-<slug>.html
  (slugs: purple-haze, comfortably-numb, pride-and-joy, smells-like-teen-spirit)
- Each lesson: the real rig with hedged claims, a budget recipe with amp-settings
  ranges, the theory, original tab drills, and the official recording playable
  on the page.
- The author wrote and typeset the whole book himself with dependency-free Python.
- There is a paid book ($19.99 unlock) but posts NEVER link the paywall or mention
  the price. If relevant: "there's a paid book behind it, but these four are just free."

## Deliverable
One file: fragments/growth/reddit-posts.md [NEW] containing three posts:

1. r/guitarpedals — links lesson-purple-haze.html. Angle: the Fuzz Face/Octavia
   signal chain, what is documented vs lore, "ranges not gospel."
2. r/guitarlessons — links lesson-smells-like-teen-spirit.html. Angle: a genuinely
   beginner-friendly full lesson, dynamics as the skill.
3. r/guitar — links lesson-comfortably-numb.html. Angle: the Gilmour bend-and-hold
   vocabulary and why the tone is mostly hands.

Per post: 3 title options (under 300 chars, no clickbait, no ALL CAPS), the post
body (120-250 words, first person, discloses "I wrote this"), and 3 short reply
drafts for likely comments (a skeptic, a gear question, a "why free" question).

## Hard rules
- No exclamation points, no em dashes, no "game-changer/unlock/master" hype words.
- No invented facts, stats, or testimonials. No urgency language.
- Each post reads like a musician sharing work, not a marketer placing content.

## Acceptance criteria (self-check)
1. Three posts, each with 3 titles, 1 body, 3 replies.
2. Authorship disclosed in every body. No price, no paywall link.
3. Only facts from the list above. Zero hype vocabulary.
4. Only fragments/growth/reddit-posts.md written.
