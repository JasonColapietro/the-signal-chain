# Brief w12 — short-form clip scripts (TikTok / Shorts / Reels)

Read `AGENTS.md` in the workspace root first. Its voice and honesty rules apply.
This brief overrides its file-format section: deliverables are content drafts.

## Job
Write four short-form video scripts, one per free lesson, in a repeatable format:
"the actual <song> settings." Each video is 25-35 seconds, built around a
screenshot of the lesson's amp-panel graphic (knob diagram with honest ranges)
plus the presenter talking or captions over B-roll. The hook is honesty: most
settings videos online invent precision; these show documented ranges and say
what is lore.

## Inputs (read for facts)
- fragments/reference/lessons.html (each lesson's rig, recipe ranges, and hedging language)

## Deliverable
One file: fragments/growth/clip-scripts.md [NEW] with four scripts
(Purple Haze, Comfortably Numb, Pride and Joy, Smells Like Teen Spirit), each:

1. HOOK (first 2 seconds, spoken line + on-screen text, under 8 words each).
2. Beat-by-beat script: 4-6 beats with timestamp ranges, spoken words, and the
   exact visual per beat (one beat MUST be the amp-panel screenshot; one beat
   MUST name what is lore vs documented).
3. CTA line: "full lesson free at guitar solutions, no email" phrasing variants
   (2 options, natural spoken English).
4. Caption text with 4-6 hashtags (real, existing guitar hashtags, no invented ones).

## Hard rules
- Settings quoted must MATCH the ranges in lessons.html exactly. Never sharpen
  a range into a single number.
- No exclamation points in on-screen text or captions, no em dashes, no hype
  words, no "secret settings" framing. The anti-secret stance IS the hook.
- Spoken lines must be sayable in the time given (2.5 words/second max).

## Acceptance criteria (self-check)
1. Four scripts, each with hook, 4-6 beats, 2 CTA variants, caption.
2. Every quoted setting matches lessons.html verbatim or as a faithful range.
3. Total spoken words per script fits 35 seconds at 2.5 words/sec (max ~87 words).
4. Only fragments/growth/clip-scripts.md written.
