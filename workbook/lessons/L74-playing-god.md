# Lesson 74 — “Playing God” · Polyphia (Tim Henson)

## At a Glance

- **Artist:** Polyphia (Tim Henson)
- **Song:** “Playing God”
- **Album:** *Remember That You Will Die* (2022)
- **Section:** NOW
- **The tone in one sentence:** A nylon-voiced electric guitar through modern processing, where classical brightness, trap-era precision, and modeler polish all live in the same part.
- **Difficulty:** Advanced
- **Key & tuning:** E-centered minor and harmonic-minor color for this lesson; standard tuning. The song's production and arrangement are dense enough that the technique matters more than naming one static scale.

## The Rig & Signal Chain

This is not a traditional nylon-string lesson and not a traditional shred lesson. The guitar voice is deliberately hybrid: percussive like a classical guitar, edited like a modern production, and processed like an electric rig.

- **Guitar:** Henson is commonly associated with Ibanez nylon-string electric instruments in this era, including the TOD10N family and prototypes around it. The exact production chain is less important than the nylon-voiced, pickup-driven behavior.
- **Processing:** Neural DSP and modeler-style processing are closely associated with Henson's modern tones. Expect compression, EQ, ambience, and a clean amp or DI-like platform rather than a roaring amp.
- **Gain:** Very little traditional distortion. The "size" comes from compression, double-tracking, arrangement, and polished top end.
- **Technique:** Hybrid picking, left-hand slurs, percussive thumb movement, and harmonics are part of the tone. The rig will not make the part sound correct if the hands are uneven.

## The Tone Recipe

**Record-side target:** a nylon-voiced electric signal into compression, clean modeling, bright EQ, and a controlled room or plate-style ambience. The note front should be clear, and the low end should be lighter than a steel-string electric.

**Affordable path:**

- **Guitar:** Nylon-string electric if available. Otherwise use a clean electric with the tone rolled back slightly, or an acoustic-electric modeler patch.
- **Compression:** Medium compression to even out plucks and taps.
- **Amp/modeler:** Clean amp, acoustic sim, or DI-style patch with EQ.
- **Ambience:** Short room or plate, not a huge wash.

**Starting points (knobs out of 10):**

- Compression: **4–6**, enough to even notes without flattening them.
- Amp gain: **0–2**.
- Bass: **3–5**. Mids: **5–6**. Treble: **6–8**.
- Reverb: **2–4**.
- Picking: **hybrid**, with thumb and fingers close to the strings.

The target is fast and clean, but not sterile. You should still hear the finger attack.

## What's Going On Musically

"Playing God" is internet-era virtuosity in the best sense: the guitar is not just playing fast notes. It is carrying melody, percussion, arrangement, and production identity at once.

The harmonic flavor borrows from **minor**, **harmonic minor**, and modal colors that sound Spanish or classical without becoming a straight classical piece. The technique leans on **hybrid picking**: the pick handles some attacks while the middle and ring fingers pluck higher strings. That lets one player create harp-like spacing and quick string skips.

The rhythmic grid is just as important. The notes often sit with programmed-drum precision, but the nylon attack keeps the part human. That tension is the modern lesson.

## The Drills

Original exercises for the technique.

**Drill 1 — Hybrid-Picked Cascade.** Target: **80 BPM**. Make pick and fingers equal.

```
Use pick on G, middle on B, ring on e.

e|--------7-----------5--------|
B|-----8-----8-----7-----7-----|
G|--9-----------7--------------|
D|-----------------------------|
A|-----------------------------|
E|-----------------------------|
   p  m  a  m   p  m  a  m
```

*Builds:* even string skipping. If the finger notes jump out too loudly, lower the hand and relax.

**Drill 2 — Clean Slur Grid.** Target: **96 BPM**. Combine hammer-ons and picked notes without losing the beat.

```
Light compression, no gain.

e|----------------5h7p5--------|
B|----------5h7h8-------8p7----|
G|--4h5h7-------------------7--|
D|-----------------------------|
A|-----------------------------|
E|-----------------------------|
```

*Builds:* left-hand clarity. Every slur should have a front edge.

**Drill 3 — Percussion Between Chords.** Target: **72 BPM**. Treat muted strokes as part of the pattern.

```
Thumb or pick rake the muted hit.

e|--7--x--7---8--x--8---------|
B|--8--x--8---7--x--7---------|
G|--9--x--9---8--x--8---------|
D|----------------------------|
A|----------------------------|
E|----------------------------|
```

*Builds:* clean chord control and rhythmic muting. The x has to land as confidently as the chord.

## Make It Yours

You do not need to copy the whole Polyphia vocabulary to use the lesson. Start by writing one clean chord shape, then add one string-skipped melody note and one muted percussion hit. Keep the gain out of the way until the part works. Modern virtuosity is not only speed; it is arrangement density under control.

> **Listen For**
>
> - **0:00–0:20:** nylon-like attack presented with electric precision.
> - **Main motif returns:** string skips that sound arranged, not improvised.
> - **Percussive gaps:** muted hits acting like drum notes.
> - **Harmonic details:** bright pings that would vanish under heavy gain.
> - **Dense sections:** the tone staying clean enough for every small note to read.
